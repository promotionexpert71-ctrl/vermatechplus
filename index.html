The Problem

Most losses aren't strategy failures.
They're discipline failures.

The same three mistakes repeat across almost every losing trading account — and none of them are about picking the wrong pair.

01

Late entries, early exits

By the time the trend is obvious enough to feel safe, most of the move is already gone.

02

Signal noise

Every small wick looks like a reversal in the moment — most of them aren't.

03

Inconsistent risk

Position size that changes with confidence, not with the market, is how one bad week erases five good ones.

How It Works

One pipeline, run the same way every time

Four checks happen before Meet Trader places a single order — in this order, without skipping a step.

01

Confirm the trend

A 200-period EMA on the higher timeframe sets the bias. If price isn't aligned with it, nothing else matters yet.

H4 · 200 EMA
02

Filter the signal

A fast/slow EMA crossover triggers the setup — but only fires if RSI confirms the move isn't already exhausted.

EMA cross · RSI filter
03

Size the risk

Stop-loss and take-profit are set from current volatility (ATR), and position size is calculated to risk a fixed % of the account — never a guess.

ATR-based · % risk sizing
04

Execute and protect

Once in profit, a trailing stop moves automatically to lock in gains as the trade develops — no manual watching required.

Trailing stop
Under The Hood

Every safeguard, always on

Nothing here is optional or manual — it runs on every trade, every time.

Trend confirmation

Higher-timeframe EMA bias keeps trades aligned with the dominant direction, not against it.

RSI signal filter

Screens out setups where momentum is already stretched, before they become losing trades.

Adaptive stop-loss

Stops widen or tighten with ATR volatility — not a fixed pip value that ignores market conditions.

Fixed % risk sizing

Lot size is calculated from account balance and stop distance — the same risk logic on every single trade.

Trailing stop

Locks in profit progressively as a trade runs, without needing to sit and watch the screen.

Spread & session filters

Avoids entries during unusually wide spreads, one open position at a time, by design.
